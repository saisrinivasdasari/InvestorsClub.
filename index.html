How The Ecosystem Connects You:
Two Clear Pathways.

Whether you arrive with a breakthrough concept seeking capital, or investment capacity looking for vetted early-stage innovation, Investors Factory provides a transparent, milestone-driven pathway.

Stage 01 • Onboarding

1. Submit Your Concept

Create your builder profile and register your project with problem definition, solution, target audience, and current development stage (Idea, Validation, or MVP).

Start Submission
Stage 02 • Team Formation

2. Assemble Skills & Build MVP

Investors back execution, not just talk. Access the skilled marketplace to find engineers, designers, and domain co-founders to validate prototypes and build real traction.

Find Skilled Talent
Stage 03 • Readiness

3. Mentorship & Due Diligence

Work with ecosystem strategists to structure pitch decks, clarify milestone-based financial requirements, ensure IP safety, and achieve vetted listing status.

Explore Advisory
Stage 04 • Connection

4. Connect & Secure Capital

Your vetted opportunity is showcased to verified angels, syndicate partners, and resource providers. Receive direct enquiries and negotiate structured partnerships.
